03-COVERS / COVER CONCEPT

Concept

One of every twenty covers. No figure: the pace-line and data language carries it — diverging lines, distance rulers, intensity bars, effort dots. Three headline modes on navy and on white. ground-deep is allowed on any navy cover and counts as navy for the alternation; these six use flat navy so the checkerboard reads cleanly.
