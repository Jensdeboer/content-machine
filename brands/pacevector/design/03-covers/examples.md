# Cover examples

The eight cutout covers from the original brand system board, exported at 540 x 675 (half of the 1080 x 1350 canvas). Where these contradict `cover-grammar.md`, these win.

- `cover-show-up-deep.png` — Shout mode on ground-deep: SHOW behind the kicking figure, UP. in front of it, italic aside above, pill call to action bottom-left.
- `cover-two-voices-light.png` — Sentence mode on the light tint: italic "Run" over bold "slower" over italic "to race faster", full-stride figure cut by the bottom edge.
- `cover-portrait-white.png` — Number mode on white: the figure bleeds off the left and bottom, 500 set right-aligned over the shoulder with a mono unit under it.
- `cover-monument-navy.png` — Number-adjacent monument on flat navy: REST behind the seated figure, italic "is training." in front, bottom-right.
- `cover-monument-deep.png` — Shout mode on ground-deep: italic lead-in, 5AM across the middle, crouched figure on the bottom edge, bold payoff bottom-right.
- `cover-numeral-light.png` — Number mode on the light tint: a single 5 at monument scale, figure bleeding off the right, headline finishing the sentence bottom-left.
- `cover-statement-navy.png` — Sentence mode on flat navy: mirrored low-angle figure bleeding off the left, right-aligned italic and bold stack in front.
- `cover-numerals-white.png` — Number mode on white: 80/20 behind a full-stride figure centred on the bottom edge, headline bottom-left.
