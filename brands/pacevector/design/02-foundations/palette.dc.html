02-FOUNDATIONS / PALETTE

Colour roles

Thirteen roles, mapped from the board's named colours. The board name is carried in each role's source field in tokens.json. Values live only in tokens.json.

groundSOURCE · WHITE
DEFAULT SLIDE GROUND
ground-altSOURCE · LIGHT
SECONDARY GROUND · IMAGE PANEL
ground-navySOURCE · VECTOR NAVY
FULL-FRAME NAVY SLIDES AND COVERS
ground-deepSOURCE · DEEP
COVER GROUND ONLY · TWO-STOP NAVY RADIAL
inkSOURCE · BLACK
HEADLINES ON LIGHT GROUNDS
ink-softSOURCE · BODY COPY INK
BODY COPY ON LIGHT GROUNDS
muteSOURCE · SLATE
KICKERS · SECONDARY LABELS ON WHITE
mute-invSOURCE · SLATE, INVERTED
KICKERS · SECONDARY LABELS ON NAVY
lineSOURCE · RULE
HAIRLINES · DIVIDERS · GRID
accentSOURCE · VECTOR NAVY
ACCENT GROUND · CHIPS · MARK
accent-inkSOURCE · VECTOR NAVY
ACCENT AS TYPE ON LIGHT GROUNDS
on-accentSOURCE · WHITE
TYPE AND MARK ON ACCENT
signalSOURCE · SIGNAL
COVERS ONLY · AT MOST ONE ELEMENT PER COVER · NEVER ON A SLIDE
RULES

accent, accent-ink and on-accent are kept as separate roles even where two of them share a value: they are different jobs and may diverge. signal is for covers only, at most one element per cover, and never appears on a slide.
