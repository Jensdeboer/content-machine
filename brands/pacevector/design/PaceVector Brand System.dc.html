Brand System

v1 · Identity + Instagram carousel system

CONCEPT

The pace line. A runner's progress is a vector: a direction and a magnitude. Every element of the identity draws from that idea. The mark is a P whose bowl has been pushed forward into a vector head. Typography is set tight and forward-leaning in hierarchy, never in slant. Graphic details borrow from the technical margins of a running magazine: tick marks, split times, coordinate labels.

The contrast rule. Covers are emotion: a black-and-white runner cut from the photograph and placed on plain white or navy, oversized type behind or around the figure, a single navy accent. Information slides are clarity: white, black type, navy only where it points at something. The feed alternates between the two. That alternation, more than the logo, is what makes a post recognizable as PaceVector.

ASSUMPTIONS & NOTES

  • Type: Sora (display, geometric, slightly technical) + IBM Plex Sans (body) + IBM Plex Mono (annotations). All open-license.
  • Your six B&W cutouts are used (the baked-in checkerboard was stripped to true transparency). Covers now run across four grounds, deep navy to white, so the feed reads lighter/darker post to post while staying inside the navy family.
  • Format scoped to 4:5 carousel (1080×1350) and profile picture. Story/reel formats can be derived from the same anatomy.
  • Slides are authored at full 1080×1350 and shown scaled. Tweaks panel: toggle layout guides, change the photo treatment.
  • Cover mix (70 / 15 / 10 / 5) is demonstrated across the five example carousels and the grid preview.

TWO VOICES

Sora 800 is the statement. IBM Plex Sans Light Italic is the aside. One of each per cover, never more. The italic is always smaller than the statement.

MONUMENT WORDS

Caps or numerals only, 300–640px, tracking −6 to −8%. One per cover. The figure has to overlap it, otherwise it is just a big word. Hairline rules and mono annotations may frame it.

THE FIGURE

Always big: the figure fills at least two-thirds of the frame height. B&W, hard edge, no drop shadow, no outline. Feet meet the bottom edge; it may bleed off one or two sides. It never floats and is never small.

THE GROUND

Deep (soft light behind the figure), Vector Navy, cool Light tint or White. On dark grounds all type is white. Alternate dark and light post to post so the grid checkerboards.

SCIENCE02 / 07

Breathlessness is not an oxygen shortage.

At hard effort your blood still leaves the lungs almost fully saturated. What you feel is your body working to clear CO₂ and acid faster than it can. The limit is in the muscle, not the air.

Below it, your body clears lactate as fast as it makes it. Above it, the balance tips and the clock starts.
