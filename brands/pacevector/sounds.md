# Sounds

Weekly shortlist. Not chart-toppers — established, widely-used platform
sounds. The packet suggests one from this list; which sound went with which
deck is history and lives in state.db and posted.jsonl, never here. The rule:
never the same sound two posts running, and otherwise the one used longest
ago, unused ones first in shortlist order.

Columns are read by header name. Sound is the only one that must exist; the
others are carried along into the packet message, and can be added, dropped
or reordered.
