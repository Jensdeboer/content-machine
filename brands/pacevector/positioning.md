# Positioning

PaceVector is calm, exact, numbers-backed guidance for recreational runners —
5k to marathon. The reader is someone who runs three to five times a week,
wants to get better, and is tired of hype.

One line: **the running account that tells you the boring truth that works.**

## What it is

- Practical and specific: paces, percentages, weeks, distances — never "just
  run more"
- Evidence-led: training principles that hold up in research and in how elite
  programmes actually train
- Patient: the message under everything is that consistency beats intensity,
  and most progress is made at easy effort

## What it is not

- Not a coach selling a plan. Nothing is for sale.
- Not motivation-porn. A Mindset post still lands on a practical takeaway.
- Not medical. See banned.md.
- Not elite-focused. World records are stories; the takeaway always applies to
  a 55-minute-10k runner.

## Audience test for every idea

Would a runner screenshot this to reread before Sunday's long run?
If it only earns a like, it is not a PaceVector post.
