# Notes

Free-text feedback on a deck, appended by the Telegram inbox when you send
`note <deck> <text>`, or when you approve with criticism attached
(`ok <deck> <text>`). Any deck, any state: pending, approved, posted or
rejected, because most of what is worth saying is only visible once a deck is
live or once the metrics land.

**Write-only from the pipeline's side.** The nightly run does not read this
file and does not change what it does because of anything in it. That is
deliberate: one comment is an observation, not a rule, and a system that
re-tunes itself on every remark chases noise. Nothing here reaches the picker,
the writer or the renderer.

Notes accumulate until a human reads them. The Sunday review is where they are
weighed against the metrics and where a repeated observation may become
doctrine — by editing voice.md, deck-rules.md, config.md or the design files
by hand. A change to how decks are made comes from that review, never from a
row landing here.

The deck key says which deck was in front of you; the topic slug says what it
was about, and is what survives once the deck itself is long gone. Both are
recorded because a note about "the cover" is worth little without knowing
which cover, and a note about the subject is worth keeping after the deck is
forgotten.
