# Rejected

Appended automatically when you reject a deck with a reason, and by hand
for ideas killed in review. The idea picker never re-proposes anything here.

The slug is the match key: the picker excludes an exact slug outright, then
asks the model whether a surviving candidate is the same topic as anything
here or in the last 60 days of posted.jsonl. Prose alone was never a reliable
lookup. Use the same slug the brief carried, kebab-case.

Scope says what was rejected. Only the exact word `figure` narrows it: one
figure or angle died, the topic stays eligible, the picker is told what died
and what survived, and the Reason column carries the surviving angle and the
sources verify already found, so a future night starts from them rather than
from the dead figure. Anything else, a missing cell, an empty cell, a typo,
an old four-column row, is the whole topic, never re-proposed. A rejection
whose scope cannot be read excludes more, never less.
