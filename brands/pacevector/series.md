# Series

Five series. Deck keys are PV-NNN with the series recorded in posted.jsonl.
Rotation: no series twice within 3 posts; weights below are per rolling 20
posts and may be re-proposed by the weekly PR as performance data arrives.

## Running 101 — weight 5/20

Beginner-to-intermediate fundamentals. Easy pace, cadence, long runs,
walk-run, how to start. Example: "Why 80% of your runs should feel too easy."

## Mindset — weight 4/20

Discipline and psychology, always landing on something practical. Habit
mechanics, the 5am start, motivation vs systems. Example: "Nobody feels like
running at 5am. Run anyway."
Boundary: general habit/discipline psychology only — never mental health.

## Science — weight 4/20

Physiology explained plainly. Why legs quit before lungs, what easy running
builds, heart-rate drift, heat. Example: "Your legs quit before your lungs do."
Every Science deck cites a primary source in the caption.

## Gear — weight 3/20

Shoes and equipment, evidence over brand talk. Replacement mileage, rotation,
what actually matters in a shoe. Example: "Replace them at 500, not 800."
No brand recommendations, no affiliate anything — generic categories only.

## Mistakes — weight 4/20

Common errors and their fixes. Too fast too soon, racing every workout,
skipping sleep. Example: "5 mistakes quietly ruining your marathon."
Tone check: the reader is never mocked; the mistake is always understandable.

## Evergreen reserve

Ideas that work any week, used when a night run fails: easy-pace explainer,
shoe mileage, cadence myth, taper basics, long-run fuelling (food, not
supplements).
