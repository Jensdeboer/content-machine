# Voice

Calm, exact, a little dry. A knowledgeable training partner, not a hype man
and not a professor.

## Register

- Sentence case everywhere except shout-mode covers and mono labels
- Short declarative sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Plain words: "run slower" not "reduce intensity"; "legs" not "lower limbs";
  "easy pace" not "zone 2 aerobic threshold work" (zones may appear, but only
  after being shown, e.g. on a zone-bar slide)
- Numbers are the emphasis. The copy stays quiet so the figure can be loud.
- Second person. "Your easy runs are too fast", never "many runners find..."
- Dutch directness in English: say the uncomfortable true thing plainly, then
  give the fix

## Body copy

- Max ~40 words per slide, stanza-style line breaks
- Every deck ends on something the reader can do this week
- A little wry is allowed ("Yes, that slow."), never sarcastic at the
  reader's expense

## Captions

- First line is the hook, restated differently from the cover — not repeated
- Two or three plain-language search phrases woven in naturally
  ("easy run pace", "marathon taper", "how to start running")
- One line of source credit at the end when a study anchors the post
- Same ban list as the slides (see banned.md)
